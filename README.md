# Aggie Marine Robotics Codebase

Welcome to Aggie Marine Robotics!

## Current tasks

### Flight Controller
The submarine will use a custom Arduino flight controller to enable movement.

### Decision Making
The submarine will have a central program that will take in information and decide which tasks to attempt and actions to take.

### Communication
The submarine will communicate necessary information between its various electrical components, passing detailed directions and information between them.

### Computer Vision
The submarine will be able to identify the tasks and where they are from its cameras.

### Sonar Module
The sumbarine will use a Teledyne sonar module to determine its speed and direction, and distance from the bottom.

### Hydrophones
The submarine will use three or more hydrophones to triangulate the position of specific pingers.

### Other Sensors and Components
The submarine will process information from a variety of other sensors (pressure, temperature, etc.) and perform actions with a variety of other components (torpedoes, droppers, grabber, etc.).

### Testing and Logging
The submarine will require a way to send (and receive) detailed information back to the surface during tethered testing and record essential information whenever it is run.
